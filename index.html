Course focus

Vectors help describe the world in motion.

Welcome to our MCV4U Course Culminating Task website. This site follows Chapter 7, Applications of Vectors, from Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12.

Through lesson notes, visual models, videos, worked examples, and practice tasks, we will explore how vectors describe force, velocity, direction, and motion in real-world situations. Our goal is to build a clear understanding of dot products, cross products, and their applications in navigation and problem solving.
